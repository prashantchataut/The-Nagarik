# Comments policy

Comments stay **off** on The Nagarik until all of the following exist:

1. Cloudflare Turnstile (or equivalent) on the post form
2. Staffed moderation queue with a published SLA (e.g. first response &lt; 24h)
3. Clear community guidelines in Nepali + English on `/trust`
4. Kill switch env: `COMMENTS_ENABLED=true` only when ready

Do not enable a comments UI as a pitch prop. Peers that ship unmoderated comments create legal and trust risk the PRODUCT forbids.
